- New: Spotify (Media). Sign in with your own Spotify account in the web browser, then search for tracks, artists, albums, playlists, audiobooks and podcasts, play your liked songs and your playlists, open artist pages with popular tracks and related artists, and find similar tracks. Playback needs Spotify Premium; recording is not possible. Not on iOS and Android
- New: The Klangten browser (Media, Web browser). It reads web pages like a screen reader: browse mode with quick navigation (H heading, K link, B button, F form field, E edit field, 1 to 6 heading level and more, with Shift backwards), focus mode for filling in forms and for web applications (Alt+Space switches), sign-ins are kept, files are downloaded or played. It needs Chrome for Testing from Google once (about 200 MB); Klangten asks before downloading it. There is also a simple text browser without Chrome, on iOS and Android as well. Links in posts and messages open in the Klangten browser or in the system browser, as you choose
- New: Background playback. Radio stations, podcast episodes, Internet Archive entries and YouTube videos keep playing while you work elsewhere in Klangten: press B in the player or choose "Play in the background". F7 opens the player of the background, Shift+F7 pauses, Shift+F5 and Shift+F6 change the volume. The playlist and the background take turns
- New: On Windows and macOS, system-wide keys control the player from other programs too: Windows+F5 and Windows+F6 volume, Windows+Z and Windows+X previous and next track, Windows+Space play or pause, Windows+F7 what is playing, Windows+F8 stop, Windows+F11 microphone recording. On the Mac, Option takes the place of Windows. They can be switched off in the settings under Media
- New: R records what plays in the player (radio, podcast, file) as MP3 in the recordings folder
- New: Control+F11 records from the microphone anywhere in Klangten; a second Control+F11 saves the recording
- New: Podcast episodes and Internet Archive files remember where you stopped and offer "Continue" the next time
- New: Internet Archive (Media). Open entries by address or identifier, listen to files, continue them and download them, read the description
- New: Media catalog: "My history", "Recently rated", favourites of friends, public and group favourites, sharing your own favourites, "Enter an address" (address or catalog number) and sending a correction or note to the editors. Editors edit entries directly in Klangten
- New: Polls can be saved as drafts, edited, published later (for everyone, only through the link or for a group) and withdrawn. Group polls, browsing by author, language and group, and answers can be saved for later
- New: Notes kept only on this device, sharing a note with a group and reading group notes
- New: In the user menu, status history, the contacts of this user and a comment on your own contacts. Under Users "Special users" and "Recently changed audio avatars"; Control+F9 tells your own status history
- New: Messages to a whole Klango group, to all members or only to the moderators
- New: Share forum threads and blog posts on Mastodon
- New: Manage your Klango e-mail addresses (Account, account security): add and delete addresses, choose the sender and the sender name, set the mailbox password, read the settings for e-mail programs
- New: Import data from Klango (Tools): local notes and the Mastodon sign-in from Klango 3 and Klango 4. Klango itself stays unchanged
- New: After signing in, Klangten reads the server's message of the day
- New (Windows): Klangten can speak through any screen reader, not only NVDA: JAWS, Narrator, System Access and others (speech settings, voice "Screen reader")
- New: Start options for another data folder (--overwrite-config-path), another recordings folder (--overwrite-recordings-path) and another Klango server (--srv-host-base-url). Klangten warns before it talks to a foreign server for the first time; updates always come from the official server. When a foreign server has no valid licence, Klangten reads out its message
- Changed: Klangten has its own sound engine. The former audio library BASS, whose licence does not fit the GPL, is gone completely; Klangten plays, records and converts with free software now (miniaudio, FFmpeg, LAME). Every format played before, web radio and podcasts keep playing; tempo, pitch and the sound effects of programs work on every system, Android included. MIDI files and VST plugins are no longer available
- Changed: Translation (Control+T) goes through the Klango server instead of directly to Google
- Changed: The program files are called Klangten now as well: klangten.exe instead of elten.exe, the Task Manager shows "Klangten", and on the Mac the program inside the bundle is called Klangten. The update moves the old files, shortcuts and the autostart entry over by itself
- Fixed: Incoming calls did not ring, and outgoing calls did not announce when the other person answered
- Fixed: Voice posts in forums and voice messages sounded dull and squashed. Klangten records them at 64 kbit/s in music mode now, and the server converts them in high quality
- Fixed (Android): Voice posts, recorded thread titles and voice messages did not play, nor did Opus, FLAC and WebM files and HLS streams
