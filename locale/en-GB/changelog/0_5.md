- New (Android): Spotify. Sign in with your own Spotify account, search, playlists and playback as on the computer; playback requires Spotify Premium
- New (Android): Klangten stays connected in the background and shows new messages and notifications as system notifications, without Google services. On by default; can be switched off in the settings ("Stay connected in the background"). Klangten asks once for permission to show notifications and for the exemption from battery optimisation
- Fixed (Windows): after quitting, Klangten sometimes kept running invisibly, even for days, and held on to the sound card. The update to this version ends such leftover Klangten processes
- Fixed: almost every YouTube video reported "This video has no audio stream that can be played". YouTube accepts freshly issued addresses only after a few seconds; Klangten now waits for that, at most 15 seconds
- Fixed (Android, iPhone): microphone recordings and voice posts could only be heard on the left. They are now centred, and in stereo where the phone has a stereo microphone
- Fixed: every account was offered moderation items such as "Ban globally" in the user menu, which only administrators may use
- Safer: the text browser no longer opens links to files on the computer or to scripts. Links that start another program open only after a question; e-mail, phone and text message links still open directly
- Fixed: some special characters in descriptions from the Internet Archive were shown incorrectly
