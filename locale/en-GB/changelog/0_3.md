- New: YouTube (Media) has been rewritten. Search for videos, channels and playlists, pasted links are recognised, and Up and Down arrow step through earlier searches. Results come in pages of 50 with "next 50" at the end. Enter plays straight away with repeat on; Shift+Enter opens the action menu with quality, channel, download, favourites, copy link, audio avatar and details. History and favourites are new. Downloads keep the original audio or use any available format. The background sound is silent while a video plays
- New: The file manager (Files) has been rewritten. Enter plays audio files, reads documents aloud (TXT, HTML, DOCX, ODT, EPUB, RTF and PDF), opens text files for editing, extracts ZIP archives and opens playlists. Enter on a folder asks whether to open it or add it with all subfolders to the playlist; Right arrow opens it directly. Ctrl+O opens a file with the system program, Ctrl+I says its size, Ctrl+D the duration or the contents of a folder. Files can be converted, packed as ZIP and set as audio avatar; text files, recordings and playlists can be created
- New: The playlist (Files) keeps playing in the background after leaving the screen. Shuffle, repeat and volume are remembered, M3U and PLS can be opened and saved, and there are quick actions for play, next, previous and volume
- New: "Manage shortcuts" in the context menu of every file chooser. Name, add, rename, move and remove your own shortcuts to folders; Downloads and "Klangten playlists" are added to the defaults. A new dialog chooses folders only
- New: MP3 is built in and needs no additional software, also for "Read to file" in text fields
- Changed: The FFmpeg formats (AAC, M4A, FLAC, WMA and video) are listed directly in every format list; the "FFmpeg encoders" menu item is gone. On Windows, Klangten downloads FFmpeg the first time one of these formats is chosen; on macOS and Linux an installed FFmpeg is used
- Changed: YouTube, the file manager and the FFmpeg formats are now part of Klangten itself instead of built-in Elten programs. Data of the former programs is not carried over
- New: In voice forums the name of a new thread is recorded instead of typed, up to 10 seconds. Send becomes available once both title and post are recorded
- Fixed: Opening an entry in What's new or in the notifications, for example new messages, marked everything as read, then Klangten said there was nothing new and went back. Now the entry opens, and only what you actually view is marked as read. New blog posts open the post instead of the blog overview
- Fixed: Audio avatars sounded heavily compressed after uploading. Klangten now records them at 128 kbit/s, a fresh recording is no longer encoded a second time, and the server keeps the full bandwidth and stereo. Avatars uploaded earlier need to be uploaded again to benefit
- Fixed: Recordings in forums and messages were needlessly encoded a second time before sending and lost quality
- Changed (Android, iOS): When the on-screen keyboard opens in a field that already contains text, the text appears in the keyboard and can be corrected; confirming replaces it. In multi-line fields this applies to the paragraph under the cursor
- Fixed (Android): Emoji were damaged when typed on the on-screen keyboard
